# Mission

## What We Build

Rumbo is an application designed to be multi-platform (web and mobile), though development is currently focused solely on Android. It provides users with tools to improve their relationship with money and their personal finances.

## The goal

Redefining the objective of the app. There are two main objectives:

1. That users can have better control over their personal finances. In the end this will help them have a better quality of life, and live without worries, knowing what they do with their money.
2. That a user who comes to the application without knowing anything about personal finances has the tools to start with good habits, learn about the subject and gradually improve their relationship with money.

## Problem to solve

In Mexico there is little financial education. Rumbo wants to help people learn about personal finance. Mainly young adults, between 20 and 30 years old.

It is not intended to be a formal guide to having good finances, nor to give financial advice, much less sell financial products. It is only a conceptual guide that gives the user tools to analyze and improve their habits with money.

## Main strategy

To achieve the objectives, the main feature of the app will be a learning section, where users can learn while developing better habits.
