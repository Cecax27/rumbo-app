# Mission

## What We Build

**Rumbo** is a multiplatform application (web and Android) that allows users to quickly, securely, and easily record money transactions, define clear goals, and learn how to improve their finances.

The main objective is to enable someone with zero knowledge of personal finance to improve their personal finances.

## Who It's For

- Young people with no knowledge of personal finance
- People who already know about finance but need a simple tool

## Principles

- Everything is explained. We don't assume the user understands concepts; we explain everything.

- Open source, total transparency. We hide nothing; we are open source.

- We strive to convey approachability and quality. We are friends with our users; we want to help them; we want them to do better; we are their friends.

## What It Is NOT

- An advanced financial statistics and management app
- A simple expense tracking app
